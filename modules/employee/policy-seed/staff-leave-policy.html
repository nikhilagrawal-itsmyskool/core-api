Staff Leave, Attendance & Punctuality Policy

Effective From: 01/09/2026 · Applicable To: All Teaching & Non-Teaching Staff

Purpose & Philosophy

The policy aims to ensure regular attendance, punctuality, academic continuity, and the smooth functioning of the school.

The School recognizes that genuine illness, emergencies, and unavoidable personal or family circumstances may occasionally require leave. Such circumstances shall be considered with reasonable sensitivity and humanity.

At the same time, leave is expected to be used responsibly. Leave shall not be treated as a matter of routine convenience, and no individual’s absence should unnecessarily compromise students, colleagues, academic work, or institutional functioning.

The guiding principle of this policy is: Discipline with fairness, accountability with humanity, and uninterrupted commitment to students.

Staff members are expected to report on time and remain available for assigned academic, administrative, co-curricular, and institutional duties during working hours.

Grace Period Mechanism

Permitted Buffer (Discretionary Facility): An occasional unforeseen delay up to 7:35 AM in summers or 8:35 AM in winters may ordinarily be condoned up to a maximum of 3 instances within a calendar month.

Not a Right or Entitlement: The grace period is not a matter of right or an alternative reporting time. It is purely a discretionary facility provided by the School. The Administration/Management reserves the absolute right to withdraw, restrict, or deny this grace period facility to any staff member, or entirely for the school/department, at any time without assigning any reason. No staff member can claim the grace period as an automatic entitlement or question its applicability.

Official Reporting Time: The official reporting time strictly remains 7:30 AM (Summer) and 8:30 AM (Winter).

Progressive Consequences of Excess Late-Ins: Once the 3 permitted grace instances in a calendar month are exhausted, any subsequent late arrival, including an arrival within the otherwise permissible five-minute buffer, shall attract strict, progressive attendance adjustments and financial deductions as follows:

  • 1st Instance (Beyond Grace): It shall combine with the first 3 grace instances to be collectively adjusted as 1 Full Day Leave (Loss of Pay, where no eligible leave is available or as per service rules).
  • 2nd & Every Subsequent Late-In Thereafter: Each subsequent late arrival in the same calendar month shall directly attract 1 Half Day Absence (Loss of Pay / LOP).

Administrative & Disciplinary Review: Habitual late-coming and crossing these progressive thresholds repeatedly shall invite a formal written warning, show-cause notice, review of professional conduct, or further disciplinary action by the Principal/Director.

Exceptional Circumstances: Genuine medical emergencies, unavoidable circumstances, officially assigned duties, or verified technical/attendance-recording errors may be considered separately by the Principal/Director. Any exception shall be case-specific and shall not automatically constitute a precedent.

Leave Entitlement & Structure

Each staff member shall ordinarily have the following annual leave allocation:

  • Casual Leave (CL): 8 days
  • Medical Leave (ML): 4 days
  • Total Annual Allocation: 12 days

Leave allocation does not create an automatic or unconditional right to absence from duty. Approval shall depend upon genuine need, applicable leave balance, academic requirements, staff availability, and the overall functioning of the school. The School may require appropriate supporting documents wherever circumstances so warrant.

Probationary Staff Rule

Newly appointed staff members serving their probation period shall ordinarily accrue leave on a pro-rata basis at 1 day per completed month of service, rather than receiving the full annual allocation upfront. The leave applicable during probation shall remain subject to the employee’s appointment terms and school rules.

Carry Forward, Lapse & Encashment Rules

Unutilized Leaves: Unused CL and ML do not carry forward into the subsequent academic session and shall automatically lapse at the end of the academic session (March 31), unless otherwise specifically approved by the Management.

Encashment: Leave encashment is not permissible for standard annual leave allocation.

Maximum Leave on a Working Day

Considering the teaching staff strength and the academic requirements of the school:

  • Leave shall ordinarily not be approved for more than 2 teachers on any single working day.
  • This is a maximum operational limit, not a staff entitlement.
  • The Principal/Director may approve fewer than 2 leaves depending on examinations, classes, events, staff availability, subject requirements, or other academic/operational needs.
  • Leave shall not be approved merely because the daily limit has not been reached.
  • Priority may be given to genuine medical emergencies, unavoidable family exigencies, and critical circumstances that cannot reasonably be postponed.
  • The maximum of 2 may be further restricted by the Principal/Director whenever required for effective school functioning.

Casual Leave (CL) Regulations

CL is intended for short, occasional, and unavoidable personal needs and shall ordinarily be granted for single working days only. CL shall ordinarily not be permitted:

  • on two or more consecutive working days;
  • on a working day immediately before or after a holiday/weekly off; or
  • on working days on both sides of an intervening holiday/weekly off.

Exception: An exception may be considered by the Principal/Director in cases of genuine emergency, unavoidable family circumstances, or other compelling reasons.

These provisions are intended to discourage avoidable leave-clubbing and the misuse of CL for extending holidays or weekly offs.

Medical Leave (ML)

  • ML may be taken on consecutive working days when medically necessary.
  • Medical Certificate: ML exceeding 2 consecutive working days shall ordinarily require a formal Medical Certificate from a Registered Medical Practitioner.
  • The School may require appropriate medical documentation in other cases where circumstances reasonably warrant verification.
  • Genuine continuous medical leave shall not ordinarily be treated as holiday-clubbing merely because a holiday or weekly off falls within the medically required period.
  • Combining ML with CL may be permitted where justified, subject to approval and available leave balance.
  • The School reserves the right to seek reasonable clarification or supporting documentation where repeated or unusually frequent medical leave is observed.

Restricted Leave Days

A working day immediately before or after any of the following shall ordinarily be treated as a Restricted Leave Day:

  • a declared holiday,
  • a weekly off,
  • a festival,
  • a long weekend, or
  • any other declared non-working day.

Routine or convenience-based leave shall ordinarily not be sanctioned on such days. Exceptions may be considered in cases of:

  • genuine medical emergencies;
  • unavoidable family exigencies;
  • circumstances that could not reasonably have been anticipated; or
  • other specific circumstances approved by the Principal/Director.

Availability of leave balance by itself shall not constitute sufficient ground for approval. The purpose of this provision is to ensure adequate staff availability on working days adjoining holidays and weekly offs.

Critical Working Days

Leave shall ordinarily be restricted on days identified as critical for academic or institutional functioning, including:

  • Examination days and invigilation duties
  • Parent-Teacher Meetings (PTMs)
  • Result preparation/distribution days
  • Major school events
  • Inspections
  • Admissions-related work
  • Important academic activities
  • Staff meetings or training programmes where attendance is specifically required; and
  • Other days officially declared Critical Working Days by the school.

Only genuine emergencies or exceptional circumstances approved by the Principal/Director shall ordinarily be considered on such days. Where practicable, the School shall communicate Critical Working Days in advance.

Holiday-Club / Sandwich Provision

Where a staff member takes leave on working days immediately on both sides of an intervening holiday or weekly off, the intervening holiday/weekly off may, where permitted under the applicable service conditions and school rules, be treated as part of the continuous leave period for attendance/leave accounting purposes. However:

  • This provision shall not automatically apply to every leave situation.
  • Genuine continuous medical leave, supported by appropriate medical evidence where required, shall ordinarily be exempt.
  • Genuine emergencies and exceptional circumstances may be considered separately by the Principal/Director.
  • Repeated or deliberate holiday-clubbing may be reviewed as possible misuse of leave.

Planned Leave

  • Planned leave should be applied through the ERP at least 48 hours in advance.
  • Submission of a leave application does not constitute approval.
  • Leave shall be considered sanctioned only after formal approval by the competent authority.
  • Staff members shall provide essential academic handovers and substitution instructions wherever required.
  • Where a leave request is declined or deferred, the staff member shall report for duty unless otherwise directed.
  • For foreseeable extended leave, the School may require earlier notice depending upon the academic or operational requirements involved.

Emergency Leave

In an emergency:

  • The staff member should inform the Principal/Director before 7:00 AM in Summer or 8:00 AM in Winter, or as early as reasonably possible where circumstances prevent timely communication.
  • Wherever possible, intimation should be made through the prescribed official communication channel.
  • Failure to provide timely information may result in the absence being treated as unauthorized unless the circumstances reasonably prevented communication.
  • The staff member should complete the required ERP application/documentation at the earliest practicable opportunity.
  • Repeated use of “emergency” as a routine reason for absence may be reviewed by the Principal/Director.

Early Departure & Half-Day Leave

The official departure time is 2:50 PM in Summer and 3:50 PM in Winter.

  • Leaving before the official departure time requires prior permission.
  • Leaving up to 30 minutes before the official departure time, with prior permission, shall ordinarily be recorded as an Early Departure and shall not automatically result in Half-Day Leave.
  • Where a staff member needs to leave after completion of the 8th period but before the official departure time, permission may be granted in genuine circumstances, and the departure shall ordinarily be recorded as an Early Departure.
  • Leaving before completion of the 8th period shall ordinarily be treated as Half-Day Leave, unless specifically exempted by the Principal/Director due to the nature of the duty or circumstances.
  • Leaving the school without permission shall be treated as unauthorized early departure/absence.
  • Repeated early departures, even where individually permitted, may be reviewed as habitual non-compliance where a pattern is established.
  • Permission for early departure is an exception to normal duty hours, not a routine privilege.

Late Coming

  • The official reporting time shall remain 7:30 AM in Summer and 8:30 AM in Winter.
  • Late arrival beyond the permitted grace mechanism shall be recorded in the School ERP.
  • Repeated late-coming, even when individually minor, may be reviewed as a pattern of non-compliance.
  • Where permitted under the employee’s service conditions and applicable rules, habitual late coming may result in appropriate attendance/leave adjustment, counselling, warning, or other administrative action.
  • Continued or repeated non-compliance may lead to formal disciplinary review.

Competitive, Professional & Higher Education Examinations

The School recognizes that staff members may undertake competitive, professional, or higher education examinations. Such leave shall, however, be planned in a manner that minimizes disruption to academic functioning.

A. Single-Day / Competitive Examinations — For examinations such as TET, CTET, NET, recruitment examinations, and similar examinations:

  • Leave shall ordinarily be limited to the examination day and reasonable travel time, where justified.
  • Preparation leave shall ordinarily not be granted.
  • Prior approval shall be required wherever the examination date is known in advance.

B. Extended / University Examinations — For examinations such as MA, LL.B., University Semester examinations, or other multi-day examinations:

  • The request should ordinarily be submitted in writing at least 30 days in advance, wherever the examination schedule is published sufficiently early.
  • The official University/Board Date Sheet shall be submitted with the request.
  • The School shall assess the request considering academic requirements, staff availability, and available leave.
  • Extended examination leave shall not automatically be treated as CL.

C. Leave Beyond Available Allocation — Where examination-related absence exceeds the available approved leave allocation, the additional period may be sanctioned as Leave Without Pay (Loss of Pay/LOP), subject to approval and necessary academic substitution arrangements.

D. Half-Day Examination Provision — Where an examination falls strictly within the first or second half of the working day, Half-Day Permission may be considered, provided prior approval is obtained and necessary class/substitution arrangements have been made.

E. Documentation — An official admit card, examination schedule, or other valid examination proof shall ordinarily be uploaded with the ERP application.

Compassionate / Bereavement Leave

In the unfortunate event of the death of an immediate family member (parent, spouse, or child), up to 3 days of paid compassionate/bereavement leave may be granted separately from the regular CL/ML ledger, subject to appropriate intimation, verification where reasonably required, and approval.

Other serious and exceptional compassionate circumstances may be reviewed by the Principal/Director on a case-by-case basis. The School shall exercise reasonable sensitivity in such circumstances.

Unauthorized Absence & Disciplinary Framework

The following shall ordinarily constitute unauthorized absence:

  • Absence without prior approval, except where an emergency genuinely prevented prior communication;
  • Absence after a leave request has been declined;
  • Failure to return to duty on the approved date without authorization;
  • Leaving the school campus during duty hours without express permission;
  • Failure to follow the emergency intimation procedure without reasonable cause;
  • Any other absence determined to be unauthorized after consideration of the circumstances.

Progressive Loss of Pay (LOP) & Penalty Structure: To curb deliberate absenteeism and maintain strict institutional accountability, unauthorized absences shall attract progressive financial deductions and disciplinary actions as follows:

  • First Unauthorized Absence: Loss of Pay (LOP) for the actual days absent.
  • Second Unauthorized Absence: LOP for the actual days absent, plus 1 additional day of LOP as a penalty (Total: Actual + 1 days LOP).
  • Third & Subsequent Unauthorized Absences: LOP for the actual days absent, plus 2 or more additional days of LOP.

Depending upon the nature, frequency, duration, and circumstances of the violation, the School may also take progressive administrative or disciplinary action, which may include counselling or verbal advisory; formal written warning; show-cause notice; review of attendance and leave records; additional administrative restrictions or monitoring; formal disciplinary proceedings in cases of repeated or deliberate non-compliance; and review of continuation of service in accordance with the employee’s appointment terms, service conditions, school rules, and applicable requirements.

Repeated, deliberate, or habitual unauthorized absence may be treated as serious professional misconduct. The Management reserves the right to take appropriate action proportionate to the seriousness of the case.

(Note: Management reserves the right to review and relax these progressive penalty clauses once overall staff discipline and punctuality standards are satisfactorily established.)

Return from Leave

  • Staff shall resume duty exactly on the approved date.
  • Any extension of leave must be approved before the expiry of the sanctioned leave, wherever reasonably possible.
  • Failure to return without approval shall be treated as unauthorized absence.
  • Where circumstances prevent timely communication, the staff member shall inform the Principal/Director at the earliest possible opportunity and provide the required explanation/documentation.

Academic Handover

Before proceeding on planned leave, the teacher shall provide, wherever required: essential lesson/topic information; worksheet/homework details; pending academic work; assessment-related information; necessary substitution instructions; and any other information required for continuity of student learning, curricular, co-curricular (e.g. assembly, house and club activities) and extra-curricular activities (e.g. van/bus, morning, swing, floor duties etc.). The objective is to ensure that no student’s academic progress is unnecessarily affected by a staff member’s absence.

Official Duty

Authorized school duties shall not be treated as late or absent, provided they are properly recorded and approved. These may include official school meetings; training programmes; external seminars/workshops; board examination duties; educational visits; competitions and activities; admission-related assignments; institutional meetings; and other officially assigned duties. Such assignments should be appropriately recorded in the ERP or prescribed attendance system wherever applicable.

ERP & Attendance Record

The School ERP/attendance system shall maintain, wherever applicable: leave applications; leave approvals/rejections; biometric/Face ID attendance; reporting and departure timestamps; late-coming records; early-departure records; supporting documents; and other relevant attendance information. The ERP/attendance record shall serve as the primary administrative reference for attendance and payroll processing, subject to correction of genuine technical or recording errors. Any discrepancy in attendance records should be reported promptly to the concerned authority.

Authority of the Principal/Director

The Principal/Director shall have the final administrative authority, subject to applicable law, service conditions, appointment terms, and school rules, to approve, decline, or defer leave applications; restrict leave according to immediate academic or institutional requirements; determine appropriate arrangements for staff substitution; permit exceptions in genuine and, where necessary, verified circumstances; declare specific days as Restricted or Critical Working Days; review repeated leave, late-coming, early departure, or attendance irregularities; and initiate appropriate administrative or disciplinary action in cases of repeated misuse or non-compliance.

An exception granted in one case shall not create a precedent or an automatic right in future cases. While exercising discretion, the School shall consider the genuineness of the circumstances, academic requirements, staff availability, past attendance patterns, and overall institutional interest.

Policy Review

The School Management reserves the right to review, strengthen, relax, clarify, or modify this policy based on institutional attendance patterns; staff discipline levels; academic requirements; operational needs; changes in applicable rules or service conditions; and experience gained from implementation. The policy may be reviewed periodically and revised whenever considered necessary. Any revised provision shall become applicable from the date specified by the School.

Final Principle

Punctuality, regular attendance, professional responsibility, and commitment towards students are essential obligations of every staff member of this institution. The School expects every staff member to exercise personal responsibility, communicate honestly, plan leave responsibly, and ensure that unavoidable absence does not unnecessarily disrupt student learning or institutional functioning. At the same time, genuine illness, emergencies, bereavement, and unavoidable circumstances shall be considered with appropriate humanity and fairness.
